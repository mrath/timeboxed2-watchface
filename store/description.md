Timeboxed 2

Short description:
The classic, endlessly customizable Timeboxed watchface, rebuilt for today's Pebble watches and the new Pebble app.

Long description:

Timeboxed 2 brings the much-loved Timeboxed watchface to the current generation of Pebble watches. It keeps everything that made the original great: a big, clear clock surrounded by up to six widgets you choose yourself. It has been rebuilt to work with the new Pebble app, and adds support for the latest hardware.

WHAT'S NEW IN TIMEBOXED 2
• Support for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2, with fonts and layouts scaled for their larger screens
• Support for Pebble 2 Duo, plus all the classic Pebble watches
• Weather from the Pebble app's built-in weather service, with no API key or account to set up
• Sunrise and sunset times calculated from your location
• Stocks and crypto tickers: follow up to four symbols, such as AAPL, BTC-USD, ^GSPC or EURUSD=X, showing either the price or the daily change
• A rebuilt settings page that saves reliably, with tabs to keep things tidy and a Save button at the top and bottom
• Separate widget layouts for Default, Sleep, Tap and Shake modes. Each slot can follow the Default layout, show nothing, or show its own widget.
• Layout fixes so icons and text no longer overlap and widgets line up with your chosen alignment

FEATURES
• 7 fonts: Blocko, Blocko Big, Archivo Narrow, OSP-DIN, Prototype, LECO and Konstruct
• Left, center or right text alignment
• Up to 6 widget slots, with a 2×2 grid around the clock plus one above and one below it
• Widgets: weather, high/low forecast, feels-like temperature, sunrise, sunset, battery, seconds, compass, two extra time zones, four stock/crypto tickers, and health data (steps, distance, calories, active calories, sleep, deep sleep and heart rate)
• Different widgets after you wake up, when you tap the screen, or when you shake your wrist
• Full color customization of every element on color watches
• Celsius or Fahrenheit, miles or kilometers
• Many date formats and separators, including ISO (YYYY-MM-DD)
• 14 languages: English, Portuguese, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Danish, Turkish, Czech, Polish, Swedish, Finnish and Slovak
• Bluetooth disconnect alert, with an option to stay silent during Quiet Time
• Quick View support

COMPATIBLE WATCHES
Pebble Time 2, Pebble Round 2, Pebble 2 Duo, Pebble 2, Pebble Time, Pebble Time Steel, Pebble Time Round, Pebble Classic and Pebble Steel. Health widgets need a watch with health tracking, and heart rate needs a heart rate sensor. The second time zone, the tickers and tap/shake modes aren't available on Pebble Classic or Pebble Steel.

WEATHER
Weather comes from the weather service built into the Pebble app. If no weather is showing, enable it in the Pebble app under Settings → Plugins.
